A template is a ready-made starting point for a common automation. Each one describes the steps and integrations a script needs. When you pick one, its prompt goes into your message box. You can adjust the prompt, then send it, and the agent builds the script with you.

## Open the templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new session">
    Open a new session. Its empty chat shows a few starters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Templates for common Scripts">
    Click **Templates for common Scripts**. The templates open in the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a template">
    Filter by role (**All**, **Marketing**, **Operations**, **Product**, **Data**, **R&D**, or **Sales**), or type in **Search templates...**. Each card shows the template's steps and the integrations it uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one and send it">
    Click a template. Its prompt fills the message box. Change anything you like, such as the sources, the schedule, or where results go, then send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Not in the list? Click **Custom**, type your role, and press Enter. The message box fills with a request for script ideas for that role. Send it, and the agent suggests a few.

You can also browse templates outside the app at [script.it/templates](https://script.it/templates).

## After you send a template

- **Connect integrations.** If the script needs an integration you haven't connected, the agent asks you to connect it before the step that uses it. You can also connect integrations ahead of time from **Settings → Integrations**. See [Connecting integrations](/integrations/connecting).
- **Tailor it.** Keep chatting to change the script: "Pull from my Google Sheet instead of a CSV file", or "Also email the summary to the team after posting to Slack".
- **Run it, or put it on a trigger.** See [Inputs and running](/scripts/inputs-and-running) and [Triggers](/triggers/overview).

<Tip>
  You can also start from someone else's published script. Open its link and click **Use in Script.it** to import a copy. See [Publishing scripts](/scripts/publishing#what-visitors-can-do).
</Tip>
