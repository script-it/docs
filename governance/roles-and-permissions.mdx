A role is a named set of permissions. Everyone in an organization holds exactly one role, and the role decides what they can do there.

Your organization starts with four built-in roles. Admins can change what each one holds, create roles of their own, and give a single member exceptions. This page is the reference for all of it.

## Built-in roles

| Role | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| Owner | The one person who owns the organization. Holds every permission, and nobody can change that. |
| Admin | People who run the organization: members, workspaces, integrations, domains, and settings. |
| Billing admin | People who manage the plan, payment details, and spending, without managing members. |
| Member | Everyone else. Creates workspaces, connects and shares integrations, runs and publishes scripts. |

Two more kinds of people appear in the member list:

- **Custom roles** your organization defines. Each one starts from Admin, Billing admin, or Member. See [Create a custom role](#create-a-custom-role).
- **Guests**, people outside the organization who were invited into one of its workspaces. See [Guests](#guests).

## Permissions reference

These are the permissions you see in **Settings → Organization**, grouped as the editor groups them. The columns show what each built-in role holds before anyone changes it.

✓ means the role holds it by default. — means it doesn't.

### Integrations

| Permission | What it allows | Owner | Admin | Billing admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Share integrations** | Share a connection's live credentials into a workspace, so everyone there can act as that account. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Share custom integration types** | Publish a custom integration definition to a workspace, or to everyone in the organization. Recipients connect their own credentials. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Manage integration editors** | Give or take away edit rights on a connection or a custom integration type. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Connect and manage integrations** | Connect accounts, change their credentials and labels, and edit custom integration types and their permission policies. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Manage integration permissions** | Set or clear the Integration calls of members and roles, your own included. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Manage members' integrations** | See every member's connections and custom integration types, disconnect or delete them, and take ownership of a custom type. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |

### Workspaces

| Permission | What it allows | Owner | Admin | Billing admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Have a private workspace** | Keep a Private workspace only you can open. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Create workspaces** | Start a shared workspace the organization owns and pays for. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Share workspaces with everyone** | Open one of your workspaces to every member of the organization, including people who join later. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Invite people outside the organization** | Invite someone who isn't a member into an organization workspace, as a guest. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **See every shared workspace** | List every shared workspace the organization owns, joined or not. A listed workspace gives no access to it. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Join any shared workspace** | Join any shared workspace the organization owns as an Editor, without an invitation, or look at its members and scripts without joining. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Move workspaces between accounts** | Move a workspace out of the organization that owns it. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |

### Scripts and sandbox

| Permission | What it allows | Owner | Admin | Billing admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Publish scripts publicly** | Put a script on a public link that needs no sign-in. See [Publishing](/scripts/publishing). | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Use third-party agents** | Drive your sandbox from a third-party agent — the `scriptit` CLI, or an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude. See [CLI](/developers/cli) and [AI assistants](/channels/ai-assistants). | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Create and edit triggers** | Create, change, and turn on triggers. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Hide the Scripts tab** | A requirement, not a permission. See [Requirements](#requirements). | — | — | — | — |

### Usage and billing

| Permission | What it allows | Owner | Admin | Billing admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **See per-member usage** | Read the usage report broken down by person, including who connected which integrations. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **See billing** | Open the plan and spending screens. | ✓ | — | ✓ | — |
| **Manage billing** | Change the plan, payment details, and spending controls, and download invoices. | ✓ | — | ✓ | — |

### People and organization

| Permission | What it allows | Owner | Admin | Billing admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Manage members** | Invite people, change roles, set individual permissions and usage caps, and remove members. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Manage verified domains** | Add or remove the domains whose addresses join automatically, and set up [single sign-on](/governance/sso). | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Edit organization settings** | Rename the organization, change its icon, and edit organization-wide settings. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Manage self-hosted sandboxes** | Add, remove, and configure the machine that runs members' sandboxes on your own hardware. Applies only when self-hosted sandboxes are turned on for your organization. | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| **Delete the organization** | Owner only. | ✓ | — | — | — |
| **Transfer ownership** | Owner only. | ✓ | — | — | — |
| **Belong to the organization** | Sign in to the organization and reach the workspaces shared with you. This is membership itself. To take it away, remove the member. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Must use two-factor authentication** | A requirement, not a permission. See [Requirements](#requirements). | — | — | — | — |

### Integration calls and Agent models

Two more settings sit in the same editor, under **Integrations**. They aren't checkboxes.

- **Integration calls** limit which integration requests a member or a role may make, in the app and in everything they run. Holders of **Manage integration permissions** set them. See [Integration calls](/governance/integration-policies#integration-calls-for-members-and-roles).
- **Agent models** limit which models a member's sessions, Agents, and subagents may run. Holders of **Manage members** set them. Model calls that scripts make follow Integration calls instead. See [Models](/sessions/models).

## What turning a permission off does

Most permissions simply hide or refuse an action. A few have effects you should know about:

| Turned off | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Create and edit triggers** | The member's triggers in this organization pause, including their agent channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Teams, Slack, and email). They resume when it's turned back on. The member can still delete triggers. |
| **Have a private workspace** | The member's Private workspace is hidden, and they keep only the workspaces shared with them. Its contents are kept, and it returns when the permission is turned back on. |
| **Connect and manage integrations** | The member can't connect accounts or change connections and custom types. They can still disconnect, and connections shared with them keep working. |
| **Create workspaces** | **New workspace** no longer appears for them. |

## The rules behind every change

One rule decides who can change what: **you can only hand out what you hold, and you can only manage people who hold nothing you lack.**

- **You can grant only permissions you hold.** An Admin without **Manage billing** can't give it to anyone.
- **You can manage only members no stronger than you.** An Admin can change or remove another Admin who holds the same permissions. An Admin can't manage a Billing admin, because a Billing admin holds **Manage billing**. Nobody but the Owner can act on the Owner.
- **You can give only roles whose permissions you hold.** With the built-in roles, the Owner can give any role, an Admin can give Admin and Member, and a Billing admin or Member can give none.
- **Nobody changes their own permissions.** You can open your own to see what you hold. The one exception: a holder of **Manage integration permissions** can change their own Integration calls.
- **Ownership is never handed out in pieces.** **Delete the organization** and **Transfer ownership** belong to the Owner alone. Ownership moves only by [transferring it](/governance/admin-oversight#transfer-ownership).
- **The Member role never holds Manage integration permissions.** Neither does any role built on Member. Start a role from Admin or Billing admin to grant it.

## Requirements

Two entries in the editor bind a member instead of empowering them:

- **Must use two-factor authentication.** The member signs in with an authenticator app. Until they set one up, they're asked to before they can continue, whichever account they have open. See [Two-factor authentication](/account/security#two-factor-authentication).
- **Hide the Scripts tab.** The member's sidebar lists sessions only. They keep their scripts and the ones shared with them, and the agent still uses them.

No role has a requirement by default. Anyone with **Manage members** can set one on a role or on a member they can manage. Requirements never apply to the Owner.

## Change what a role holds

Changing a role changes it for everyone who holds it, right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the roles">
    Go to **Settings → Organization** and select **Roles**. You need **Manage members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    The list shows the built-in roles and your organization's own, with how many people hold each. You see only roles you can give.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change its permissions">
    Check or clear permissions. A badge marks each one that differs from the role's standard set: **Added**, **Removed**, or **Required**. A lock means you can't change that entry; hover it to see why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save role**. Everyone with the role gets the change at once. Exceptions set on individual members stay theirs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A dot next to a built-in role means your organization changed it from the standard set. To undo your changes, select **Use the *role* standard set**.

## Create a custom role

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new role">
    In **Settings → Organization → Roles**, select **New role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a starting point">
    Enter a name, then choose what it starts from under **Start from**: Member, Admin, or Billing admin. It starts with what that role holds in your organization today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it, then set its permissions">
    Select **Create role**. Change its permissions, then select **Save role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it to people">
    Choose it in the role picker of the member list, in an invitation, or in a [single sign-on role rule](/governance/sso#roles-from-groups).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can rename a custom role at any time. You can delete it only once nobody holds it, no pending invitation offers it, and no single sign-on rule gives it.

## Give one member an exception

Sometimes one person needs more or less than their role.

1. In **Settings → Organization**, find the member in the member list.
2. Select the permissions button next to their name.
3. Check or clear permissions, or change their Integration calls or Agent models.
4. Select **Save permissions**.

The member's row now shows **Custom**. Their exceptions stay when their role is edited, and follow them until their role changes or they leave. Changing their role clears their exceptions, except requirements such as two-factor authentication. To clear them yourself, select **Reset to the *role* role**.

## Assign roles

- **When you invite someone,** pick their role in the invitation. See [Invite members](/account/organizations#invite-members).
- **For an existing member,** change the role in the member list. You can select several members to change them together.
- **From your identity provider,** let [single sign-on role rules](/governance/sso#roles-from-groups) set roles from groups.

In every case you can give only a role whose permissions you hold.

## Guests

A guest is someone outside your organization who was invited into one of its workspaces. Guests appear in the member list as **Guest**, with the workspaces they were let into.

- Guests hold no organization permissions.
- Their work runs on their own account and their own credits, so you can't set a usage cap for them.
- They reach the workspace they were invited to, and the connections shared into it.

Inviting a guest needs **Invite people outside the organization**, which only Owners and Admins hold by default. Inviting someone who is already a member, or whose email is on one of your [verified domains](/account/organizations#configure-domain-auto-join), doesn't count as inviting a guest. To take a guest's access away, remove them from the workspace.
