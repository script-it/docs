Owners and Admins can see and act on what the organization owns, including workspaces they never joined and integrations other members connected. Each action below needs a specific permission. Owners and Admins hold all of them by default, and you can give them to a [custom role](/governance/roles-and-permissions#create-a-custom-role).

| You want to | Where | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| See every shared workspace | **Settings → Sharing → Workspaces** | **See every shared workspace** |
| Look inside a workspace, or join it | **Settings → Sharing → Workspaces** | **Join any shared workspace** |
| See and act on members' integrations | **Settings → Organization → Members' integrations** | **Manage members' integrations** |
| See usage by person | **Settings → Usage** | **See per-member usage** |
| Change what a member can do | **Settings → Organization**, member list | **Manage members** |
| Move a workspace to another account | **Settings → Sharing → Workspaces** | **Move workspaces between accounts** |
| Hand over the organization | **Settings → Organization**, organization menu | Owner only |

## See every workspace

Open **Settings → Sharing → Workspaces** with the organization active. Below your own workspaces, **Other workspaces in *organization*** lists every shared workspace the organization owns that you haven't joined, with its member count.

A listed workspace gives you no access to it. To go further:

- **Look without joining.** Select the workspace's name. You see its members and its scripts, read-only, marked "You're viewing without joining."
- **Join.** Select **Join**. You become an Editor of the workspace, like any member invited with **Can edit**.

Private workspaces never appear here.

## Manage members' integrations

Open **Settings → Organization** and expand **Members' integrations**. It lists what everyone in the organization has built and connected. Use **Show integrations of** to narrow it to one person.

### Custom integrations

Each custom integration type shows who made it, how many people connected it, and how far it's shared: **Shared with everyone**, in some workspaces, or **Not shared**. Its **⋮** menu offers:

- **Take ownership.** You become the type's owner and can edit, share, or delete it. If the person who made it is still a member, they keep using it and stay connected, but can no longer change it. You can also share it with everyone in the organization in the same step.
- **Delete.** Deletes the type for everyone and disconnects everyone who connected it. Scripts that call it stop working until someone sets it up again.

### Connected integrations

Connections are grouped by the member who connected them. Select **Disconnect** to remove that member's saved credentials. Scripts and triggers that use the connection stop working until the member connects it again.

You can disconnect a member's connection, but you can't take it over. The credentials belong to the member's own account at the provider.

## Control what a member can do

From the member list in **Settings → Organization**, open a member's permissions to turn individual permissions on or off. The ones admins most often change:

| Turn off | To |
|---|---|
| **Create workspaces** | Keep someone working only in the workspaces you share with them. |
| **Have a private workspace** | Remove their Private workspace. Its contents are kept and return if you turn it back on. |
| **Create and edit triggers** | Stop their automations. Their triggers in the organization pause, including their agent channels, until you turn it back on. |
| **Connect and manage integrations** | Stop them connecting new accounts. Connections shared with them keep working. |
| **Share integrations** | Stop them sharing their credentials into workspaces. |
| **Share workspaces with everyone** | Stop them opening workspaces to the whole organization. |
| **Publish scripts publicly** | Keep scripts off public links. |

You can also require things of a member: **Must use two-factor authentication**, or **Hide the Scripts tab** for people who should only chat. And you can limit their [Integration calls](/governance/integration-policies#integration-calls-for-members-and-roles) and Agent models.

To change it for everyone with a role, change the role instead. See [Roles and permissions](/governance/roles-and-permissions).

## Remove a member

In the member list in **Settings → Organization**, select the remove button (the trash icon) on the member's row. You can remove only members who hold nothing you lack, and never the Owner.

Before you confirm, Script.it shows what the removal stops. When you remove a member:

- They lose access to the organization immediately.
- Their triggers in the organization are turned off.
- Their premium connections in the organization, such as LinkedIn or WhatsApp accounts, are disconnected, and their monthly charges stop.
- They lose the access they were given directly to the organization's workspaces, connections, and custom integration types.
- Their environment in the organization stops.

## Move a workspace to another account

Moving a workspace takes it out of the organization that owns it, into another account you belong to.

1. Open **Settings → Sharing → Workspaces**.
2. Open the workspace's **⋮** menu and select **Move workspace**.
3. Choose the **Destination account**, confirm, and select **Move workspace**.

Scripts, triggers, and the sessions those triggers created move together. Other sessions stay in the source account.

To move a workspace you need all of these:

- You're an Editor of the workspace yourself, not only through the organization.
- You hold **Move workspaces between accounts** in the account that owns it.
- You're a member of the destination, with **Create workspaces** there.
- Everyone who owns a trigger in the workspace already has access to the destination.

A Private workspace can't be moved.

## Transfer ownership

An organization has exactly one Owner. Only the Owner can hand it over.

1. In **Settings → Organization**, open the organization's **⋮** menu and select **Transfer ownership**.
2. Choose the new owner from the organization's members.
3. Select **Transfer ownership**.

The new owner gets every owner permission, including billing. You become an Admin. Deleting the organization and transferring it again move to the new owner.

Invite another member first if you're the only one.
